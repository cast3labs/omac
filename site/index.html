Omac

Keyboard-driven tiling for macOS that works with Omarchy's keybindings.
One app. Nothing else to install.

A tiling window manager for macOS. Your windows arrange themselves side by side instead of piling up, and you move between them with the keyboard.

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/evanscastonguay/omac/main/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later — nothing else. Then allow Omac under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Every way to install, or download the app. Free to use, under these terms.

  • One native Swift app
  • No change to System Integrity Protection
  • Nothing else installed
  • Signed and notarized by Apple

Omac never sends anything without asking: your settings, your windows and your clipboard history stay on your Mac.
