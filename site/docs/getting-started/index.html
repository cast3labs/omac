Getting started

The Omac key is Left ⌘. Right ⌘ still works as a normal ⌘, so ⌘C, ⌘S and ⌘Tab are untouched.

Press What happens
Left ⌘ K Shows every shortcut
Left ⌘ Return Opens a terminal
Left ⌘ Shift Return Opens a browser
Left ⌘ Shift Ctrl A Opens your AI coding agent
Left ⌘ Shift F Opens Finder
Left ⌘ ← ↑ → ↓ Moves between windows
Left ⌘ Shift ← ↑ → ↓ Swaps a window with its neighbour
Left ⌘ 1 … 0 Switches to workspace 1–10
Left ⌘ Shift 1 … 0 Sends the window to that workspace
Left ⌘ J Turns a split from side by side to stacked, and back
Left ⌘ Shift Space Lets a window float freely
Left ⌘ Shift L Switches to the scrolling layout, and back
Left ⌘ ⌥ Space Opens the command palette
Left ⌘ Ctrl V Opens the clipboard history
Left ⌘ Shift Ctrl Space Opens the theme menu
Left ⌘ Ctrl F Full screen, and back
Left ⌘ Ctrl T Copies the text in an area of the screen
Left ⌘ Ctrl ⌥ S Starts the screensaver

Dictation (hold Left Ctrl fn) and read aloud (⌥ Esc) are off until you turn them on in Omac's menu → Speech.

To stop it: Left ⌘ Ctrl ⌥ Esc pauses the tiling; press it again to resume. omac quit quits Omac, and quitting puts every hidden window back on screen.

Every feature and its key: Features. Every setting: omac.toml.
