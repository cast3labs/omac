The one-line installer, or read the script first →

  1. Unzip and drag Omac into Applications, then open it.
  2. Allow Accessibility once, when macOS asks.
  3. Press Left ⌘K to see every key.

Closed source, notarized by Apple · No account · Omac never sends anything without asking

Why does Omac need Accessibility? Is it safe?

macOS lets an app move other apps' windows only through Accessibility, so every window manager asks for it. You allow it once. Omac is closed source, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which macOS checks before it opens. It changes nothing in System Integrity Protection and runs nothing as root.

Does it send anything?

Not unless you tick a box. The first time it opens, Omac asks two questions, both unticked: a daily count with no identifier, and update checks. Your windows, settings and clipboard history stay on your Mac. Every connection Omac can make.

How do I become a founder?

Install Omac and open it. There is no sign-up: Omac notes the day it first opens on your Mac, and that date is your founder pass. Nothing is sent. In return, tell us what breaks: omac@ghostype.ca or an issue.

Do my usual shortcuts still work? Do I need Omarchy?

⌘C, ⌘V and ⌘Tab work with either ⌘. Where Omac uses the left ⌘, the right ⌘ still does what your apps expect, such as right ⌘ Shift 4 for a screenshot. You don't need Omarchy: Omac borrows its keybindings, and it is not made by or affiliated with Omarchy.

How do I pause or remove it?

Left ⌘Ctrl⌥Esc pauses the tiling; press it again to resume. Quitting Omac puts every window back on screen. To remove it, follow Uninstall.
